What was the original Antikythera mechanism?

Discovered in an ancient Greek shipwreck around 1901, the Antikythera mechanism (c. 100–150 BC) is considered the world's oldest analog computer. It was an ingenious bronze gear system used to track astronomical cycles, predict eclipses, and model the positions of the sun, moon, and planets.

Modernkythera shows the current moon phase, the next full moon with its traditional name (Wolf Moon, Hunter's Moon, Harvest Moon and more), solar and lunar eclipses, the sun and moon in the zodiac, planet positions, the Metonic and Saros cycles, Wheel of the Year festivals such as Samhain, Yule and Ostara, and daylight length for your location.
